The feature catalogue

Every part of the book, and the world behind it.

Worldscape is built for long projects. Here is what you can do today, grouped by the job it helps with. Everything on this page ships in the app now.

Write

WorldSkript

Pages

A manuscript that looks like a book

Choose from nine book sizes and eleven typefaces, then tune text size, spacing, margins, chapter openers and paper tone with a live preview.

Structure

Chapters and a self-building index

Add a chapter and it lands in the contents on its own. Extra pages such as a prelude, dedication or map sit in front of chapter one.

Margins

Notes and event flags

Pin a sticky note to any passage, or flag a key moment with a dot in the margin. Jump around a long draft from the rail on the left.

Attention

Focus mode

Dim the menus and keep only the page in front of you. Turn spell check and your own dictionary on or off as you like.

Research

Citations and your own dictionary

Add sources by hand or import them from Zotero, and the bibliography is built for you. Teach the app the words only your world uses.

Layout

Images on the page

Place pictures in the text or on extra pages, then resize, align and caption them where they sit.

Plan

Worldbook

World

Characters, places and lore

Keep a profile for every person, city, faction, magic system and object, and record how a character changes from chapter to chapter.

Connections

Relationships you can see

A graph shows who and what is connected, and how those links shift as the story moves. Filter it by entity or by chapter.

Order

Storyboard and timeline

Arrange arcs, chapters and events, then zoom out to see chapter lengths and turning points along one line.

Time

Calendars, including invented ones

Use the everyday calendar or build your own, with custom months, weeks and named days, and pin events to it.

Find

Search with tags

Search everything you have written about your world, and narrow it with #tags.

Inspiration

MoodBoard

Collect images, notes and colour swatches on a free canvas. Link a board to a character or place, and let the palette build itself from your pictures.

Keep it safe

Your work, your computer

Versions

A history for every chapter

Save a version yourself or let the app do it. Compare any earlier version with the current text and restore it if you want it back.

Second chances

The Scrapyard

Set a chapter aside instead of deleting it. Bring it back to its old place or put it somewhere new, with its history intact.

Copies

Backup and restore

Save a copy of a project whenever you like, and load it back in a few clicks.

Ownership

Local, offline project folders

Every project is a plain folder you choose. Worldscape needs no account and no connection to write.

Sync

Two apps, one chapter list

A chapter created in the editor shows up in the storyboard, and the other way round. Events can be sent either way and stay linked.

Workspace

Split view and pop-out panes

Show any two tools side by side, give one the whole window, or move a pane onto a second monitor. Light, dark and high contrast are built in.

Ready to hand it over? Export your manuscript as a Word file for an editor or as an EPUB for an e-reader, straight from WorldSkript.
